ALVANDCODE — logo files guide
================================
Put your logo files in THIS folder (assets/img/):

1) logo.png  — your personal logo (nav bar + footer + phone icon)
   - Format : PNG (JPG also works, but PNG keeps edges clean)
   - Shape  : SQUARE 1:1 (e.g. crop just the blue "A" symbol from your banner)
   - Size   : at least 512 x 512 px
   - Tip    : your banner is wide (A + Alvand text). For the small 44px
              nav badge a square crop of the "A" looks far better than
              the squeezed full banner.

2) favicon.png — browser tab icon (can simply be a COPY of logo.png)
   - Format : PNG, square, at least 64 x 64 (512 x 512 recommended)

Until you add these files the site automatically shows the default
gradient "A" badge instead of a broken image — nothing will look broken.

After adding the files: hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5) and push to GitHub.
